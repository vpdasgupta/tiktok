How do I work out price per tag?

Price check

  • Price per tag = the label's price (plus any add-on, like a guard) ÷ the number of paper tags it replaces.
  • Compare it with your price ceiling (from the price ladder on page 2).
  • Paper and best practices count as under, since there's no new hardware to buy.
  • Anything over your ceiling can't pay back.

Three price bands

  1. Underbelow half your ceiling
  2. Nearhalf to all of your ceiling
  3. Overabove your ceiling

Ceiling fans (ceiling about $17)

  • $10 label ÷ 1 tag = $10: near
  • $10 label ÷ 2 tags = $5: under
  • $25 label ÷ 2 tags = $12.50: near
