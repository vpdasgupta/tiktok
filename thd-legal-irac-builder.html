Issue: Does Illinois' biometric privacy law (BIPA) require Home Depot to get written consent before its Illinois stores use fingerprint time clocks for hourly workers?

Rule: BIPA requires a company to get a person's written consent before collecting their fingerprint or other biometric data.

Application:
Guess: Home Depot's Illinois stores use time clocks that scan workers' fingerprints.
Guess: New hires don't sign a written consent form for those scans.

Conclusion: If the time clocks scan fingerprints and workers haven't signed a consent form, then Home Depot likely isn't meeting BIPA's consent requirement in Illinois.

Notice that both guesses stay labeled, and the conclusion says which guess it depends on.
