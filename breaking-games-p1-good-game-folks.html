Tagline

Good Game, Folks.

The why

“Good game” is the true north

“Good game” is our true north. Not only do we produce good quality games, but we want to create games that make people feel good. We also champion moments of light-hearted fun, connection, and good sportsmanship that the phrase “good game” implies.

The why

A nod to sports and simpler times

The phrase “Good game” also has ties to sports and Americana. It’s a reminder of simpler times, team sports, and greeting people with mutual respect. It creates instant nostalgia.

The why

“Folks” makes it warm

Ending with “folks,” drives home the nostalgia that “good game” starts. It’s something straight out of a 90’s broadcast or the host of a game show. It’s also immediately warm. “Folks” are neighbors, friends, and people who you have things in common with.

The why

A sneaky way to say who they are

“Good game folks” is also a sneaky self-identifier for Breaking Games. When you need a good game, we’re the folks you come to. It reminds the reader that there are many hands involved in game production – an entire community and support system that aspiring game designers can be a part of.
