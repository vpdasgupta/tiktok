SEC EDGAR: Key Terms

Six words the whole ownership check hangs on. Skim them, then keep this open while you check who owns a company.

Private company
Owned by its founders, employees, and private investors; its shares are not traded on a public stock market.
Public company
Its shares trade on a stock exchange (like the New York Stock Exchange), so anyone can buy them. Public companies must file regular financial reports with the U.S. government.
Parent company
A company that owns another company.
Subsidiary
A company owned by a parent. If the parent is public, the subsidiary is a public subsidiary — and, for us, ineligible.
SEC
The U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, the government agency that regulates public companies.
EDGAR
The SEC’s free public database of the documents (“filings”) that public companies are required to submit.
