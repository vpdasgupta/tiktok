Area 1

Pipe, Main & Aqueduct Integrity

The pipes and tunnels that carry water — finding leaks and predicting breaks before they happen.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Deep-rock aqueduct leak detection — finding leaks in large-diameter, deep-rock tunnel aqueducts and tracking surface expression over time.
  • Predictive detection of water-main breaks — using work-order, complaint, and history data to flag high-risk mains before they burst.
  • Alternative leak detection — beyond vibrational sensors, for low-pressure areas.
  • Non-destructive force-main inspection — assessing a pressurized sewer pipe without excavating or taking it offline.

Prediction counts as much as a sensor. A main-break model built from work-order history is as strong a fit as a physical leak detector — both catch failure early. A generic analytics tool with no pipe-condition angle is not.

Area 2

Treatment-Plant & Process Health

Catching plant equipment and process problems early — pumps, blowers, and the chemistry in between.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Equipment-monitoring sensors — better gas-flow metering and remote, predictive detection of abnormal equipment behavior.
  • Continuous wastewater/sewer monitoring — plus detection of contaminant anomalies.
  • Predictive maintenance for pumps & blowers — including clogs caused by wipes and debris.

A strong fit flags a plant problem before it takes equipment down — abnormal-behavior detection, predictive maintenance, contaminant alerts. General dashboards with no equipment-condition model are not.

Area 3

Coastal Resilience

Knowing the state of flood defenses in real time — before a storm, not after.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Contact sensors for floodgate status — real-time visibility into whether a gate is open, closed, or failed.
  • Crack & shift detection for flood walls — catching structural shifts (above-ground and buried) earlier than visual inspection.
  • Floodgate opening/closing tech — a better powering mechanism for roller gates weighing up to 40 tons.

A strong fit tells DEP the condition or status of a flood defense, or operates one more reliably. Generic structural-monitoring with no tie to gates, walls, or coastal defense is a weaker match.

Area 4

Water Supply & Environmental Field Monitoring

Watching the water and the environment around it — reservoirs, green infrastructure, and dams.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Cloudburst / green-infrastructure monitoring — low-cost water-level sensors and rain gauges tracking how GI performs in real storms.
  • Sample-line biofouling mitigation — keeping water-quality sample lines clear of zebra-mussel buildup without staff cleaning them every 1–2 weeks.
  • Dam-safety data acquisition — capturing and analyzing piezometer/extensometer data to catch small changes over time.

A strong fit monitors DEP's water, GI assets, or dams for early change. A consumer weather gadget or a lab instrument with no field-monitoring tie to DEP's environment is not.

Challenge 2: Asset Condition & Failure Prevention

How can DEP detect problems in its pipes, equipment, and infrastructure earlier — before failures, breaks, or downtime? Four named areas define "relevant." Condition prediction from existing data counts as much as a physical sensor.

Area 1 — Pipe, Main & Aqueduct Integrity

Deep-rock aqueduct leak detection; predictive water-main-break detection from work-order history; alternative leak detection; non-destructive force-main inspection.

Area 2 — Treatment-Plant & Process Health

Equipment-monitoring sensors; continuous wastewater/sewer monitoring and contaminant anomalies; predictive maintenance for pumps & blowers.

Area 3 — Coastal Resilience

Contact sensors for floodgate status; crack & shift detection for flood walls; floodgate opening/closing technology.

Area 4 — Water Supply & Environmental Field Monitoring

Cloudburst/green-infra monitoring; sample-line biofouling mitigation; dam-safety data acquisition.

Relevance check — answer key

Real fits: non-destructive pipe inspection (Area 1); predictive main-break modeling (Area 1); green-infra water-level sensors (Area 4); abnormal-pump-behavior detection (Area 2); floodgate contact sensors (Area 3). Not fits: a generic IoT dashboard (no condition model); a fitness wearable (no physical-infrastructure tie); an under-sink home leak-alarm (consumer scale, not DEP's mains or plants).
