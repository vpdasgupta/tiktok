Launch Your Survey

Get 15 people to take your survey

  • Aim for 15More answers make your numbers steadier.
  • Need at least 10Fewer than 10 is too few to compare 4 ads.
  • About 4 daysThen close the survey on your close date.

You don't have to wait. Start Step 3 while responses come in.

Step 1 of 4

Copy your message

In Google Forms, click Send, then the link icon. Copy the link and paste it into this message.

Message to copy

Hi! I'm doing a student project on headphone video ads. Could you take my anonymous survey? You'll watch 4 short ads and answer a few questions. It takes about 8 minutes, and you need to be 18 or older. [your survey link] Thank you!

You're on track if… Your message says how long it takes, that it's anonymous and who can take it.

If you're stuck… Make it yours. A short personal line at the start gets more replies.

Step 2 of 4

Share it in 3 places

  • Message people directlyText or DM 10 or more people you know. Send it one person at a time.
  • Post it onceTry an Instagram Story, a group chat or a class Slack or Discord. Check the group's rules first.
  • Swap with a peerOffer to take another extern's survey if they take yours.
Swap message to copy

I'm collecting responses for my ad survey (about 8 minutes, anonymous). Happy to take yours in return. Here's mine: [your survey link]

You're on track if… You shared it in 3 different places on launch day.

If you're stuck… Not sure where to post? Ask your program manager which channels other externs use.

Step 3 of 4

Send a reminder on day 2

Check your response count in the Responses tab. Then send one reminder to the people you messaged.

Reminder to copy

Quick reminder: my 8-minute ad survey closes on [close date]. If you've already done it, thank you! If you can, pass it to one friend. [your survey link]

You're on track if… You sent one friendly reminder, not five.

If you're stuck… Under 5 responses by day 2? Add a fourth place to share it.

Step 4 of 4

Close it on your close date

  1. On your close date, or once you reach 15, open the Responses tab.
  2. Turn off Accepting responses.
  3. Under 10 responses? Keep it open 1 more day, share it in one new place and tell your program manager.

You're on track if… Your survey is closed with at least 10 responses.

If you're stuck… Responses still coming in? You can start the Step 3 training now and build your Results tab once the survey closes.
