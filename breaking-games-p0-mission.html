Your mission

Who Breaking Games is

🎲

A tabletop game publisher: "an indie game publishing division of AdMagic," based in Netcong, New Jersey. It launched at Toy Fair in 2015.

It makes party, family and strategy games, like POOP, Sparkle*Kitty and Dwellings of Eldervale, and helps independent designers get their games made and sold.

Say "games," never "toys."

The new brand

🃏

A branding agency just finished it. The brand line is "Good game, folks."

It's about connection, playing face to face, and making memories around a table. The tone is fun and warm, not edgy.

There's also a new breakinggames.com.
