Measure Instagram by followers

On TikTok, engagement is measured against views. On Instagram, it's measured against followers, so big and small accounts can be compared.

Always say which one you used.

Adapt, don't copy

Your checklist for turning a TikTok into an Instagram or Facebook ad.

  • ✓Export clean from CapCut, not a downloaded TikTok with a watermark.
  • ✓Keep text in the middle of the frame. In a Reels ad, the primary text and button cover the bottom.
  • ✓Rewrite any TikTok-only words in your first line.
  • ✓Pick a cover frame that shows the game.
  • ✓Write primary text with the hook first, one extra detail, and one call to action.
  • ✓Match the Look slide of your mood board (palette marked "temporary" until the brand guide arrives).
  • ✓Label every mock-up "Concept."
