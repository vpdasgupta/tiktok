Build it in Canva

Six steps. Keep your My Screens notes open next to this.

Step 1 of 6

Copy the template

Open the template and select Use template. Canva makes your own copy.

Use template

Step 2 of 6

Make Screen 1

Select the sample screen, then Duplicate page. Change the title, words, and button to match Screen 1 in your notes.

Step 3 of 6

Make Screens 2 and 3

Duplicate and edit again. Duplicating keeps the button in the same spot on every screen.

Step 4 of 6

Add a caption under each screen

One line that starts “What this shows: …”

Step 5 of 6

Link each button

Select the button, then ••• three-dot menu, More →, then Link. Under Pages in this document, pick the next screen. Screen 3’s button can link back to Screen 1.

Tip

If only the words are clickable, select the box and its text together, choose Group, then link the group.

Step 6 of 6

Try it

Select Present and tap every button. Each one should open the screen you expect.
