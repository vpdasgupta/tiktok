Design it in Canva

Your screens, your style. Seven steps. Keep your Screen Planner open next to this.

Step 1 of 7

Start a phone-size design

On Canva’s home page, select Custom size. Type 390 wide by 844 tall, in px, then select Create new design. That’s the size of a phone screen.

390 × 844 px

Step 2 of 7

Get ideas first

Open two or three apps you use every day and look at one screen in each. Where does the main button sit? How much text is there? You can also browse Canva’s mobile prototype templates (the link is on this page).

Tip

Borrow a layout, not a look. Your words and your idea make it yours.

Step 3 of 7

Find your parts

Open Elements on the left and search for what you need:

  • Buttonsbutton, rounded rectangle
  • Iconsscan icon, cart icon, receipt icon, checklist icon, map pin icon
  • Phone detailsphone status bar, app header
  • Card screenschecklist, note card, receipt
  • CodesApps, then search QR code
  • Free onlySkip items with a crown. Those are Pro.

Step 4 of 7

Make Screen 1, then duplicate it

Build Screen 1 from your plan: a title, a few words, and one main button. Then select Duplicate page for Screens 2 and 3, so the button stays in the same spot.

Keep text big (16 px or more) and easy to read against its background.

Step 5 of 7

Label and caption each screen

At the top, a small label for whose screen it is, like “Sarah’s hdPhone.” At the bottom, one line that starts “What this shows: …”

Step 6 of 7

Link each button

Select the button, then ••• three-dot menu, More →, then Link. Under Pages in this document, pick the next screen. Screen 3’s button can link back to Screen 1.

Tip

If only the words are clickable, select the box and its text together, choose Group, then link the group.

Step 7 of 7

Try it

Select Present and tap every button. Each one should open the screen you expect.
