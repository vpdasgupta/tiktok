Sample ad, concept

You probably guessed by second 2.

The hook named a moment, and the brand and game showed up before you could scroll.

  • ✨

    Hook

    A moment that stops the scroll: a question, a problem or a surprise.

    When: First 1 to 3 seconds

    In this ad: "Rainy Saturday. Kids bored." at second 0.

  • 🏷️

    Brand and game

    The viewer can see who it's from ("Breaking Games") and which game it is.

    When: By 3 seconds

    In this ad: The box and "Breaking Games" at second 2.

  • 🎁

    One offer

    One game and one reason to buy (for example: "$10, ages 6 and up"). Not a list of everything.

    When: Middle

    In this ad: $10, ages 6 and up.

  • ➡️

    One next step

    One call to action (CTA), said out loud and shown on screen, pointing to one page on breakinggames.com.

    When: End card

    In this ad: Find your next game night, on the end card.

Wrap-up

Every ad that works has four parts

A hook, the brand and game by second 3, one offer and one next step.

Dani skips anything that looks like an ad. So an ad that works has to feel like a post for the first 3 seconds (a real moment, not a logo), and still show the brand and game by second 3.

Two more rules come with ads

🔇 Sound off

Many people watch without sound, so burn in captions and put key words on screen.

🧪 Test more than one hook

You can't know which opening wins, so you'll make 3 versions that differ only in the first 3 seconds.
