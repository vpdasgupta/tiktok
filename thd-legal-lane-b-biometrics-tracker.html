Illinois

🏛️ BIPA — Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14)

In effect strictest in the US

A business must get permission before scanning faces or fingerprints. Damages are charged per violation ($1,000/$5,000), so costs add up fast.

📌 What it does, in plain terms

The leading law in this lane, and the most likely candidate for the single focus-area law you'll map in full.

2

Federal (FTC)

🏛️ FTC v. Rite Aid (2023)

Enforcement action not a statute

A 5-year ban on Rite Aid using facial recognition, after false "shoplifter" flags fell disproportionately on minority neighborhoods.

📌 What it does, in plain terms

The canonical retail cautionary tale — choose this framing if you're drawn to enforcement rather than a law on the books.
