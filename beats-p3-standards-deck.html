Step 1 of 6

Set up section 2

  1. Open your Framework doc. Click under the Beats Creative Standards heading.
  2. Add 4 short headings: Voice, Look, Sound, Talent.
  3. Under each, type 3 lines: Do:, Don't:, Why:.

You're on track if… You have 4 areas with 3 empty lines each.

If you're stuck… Use Heading 3 for the 4 areas so they show in the outline.

Step 2 of 6

Voice

Sample (made up)
Do
Confident, short lines.
Don't
No tech-spec lists as the hook.
Why
In my survey, Sony_YouTube_01 led with tech facts and scored lowest on memorable (3.1).

You're on track if… Your Do could guide a copywriter tomorrow.

If you're stuck… Write 3 lines you think sound like Beats. What do they have in common?

Step 3 of 6

Look

Sample (made up)
Do
Bold, high-contrast, product in motion.
Don't
No static packshot opener.
Why
All 3 platforms say hook in the first 3 seconds. A still product shot gives people nothing to stop for.

You're on track if… Your Don't names something you could spot in the first 3 seconds.

If you're stuck… Pause 3 Beats ads from your repository at second 2. What do you see?

Step 4 of 6

Sound

Sample (made up)
Do
Music leads the idea.
Don't
No stock music bed.
Why
Beats sells sound, and my playbook says design for sound on and sound off. The music should prove the product.

You're on track if… Your standard says how the music and the edit work together.

If you're stuck… Ask: could this track be swapped for any other without changing the ad?

Step 5 of 6

Talent

Sample (made up)
Do
Pair stars with real fans or creators.
Don't
No star-only ad with no real voice.
Why
Beats_YouTube_01 scored 4.5 on attention but 3.0 on believable. Skullcandy_TikTok_01, a creator ad, scored 4.2.

You're on track if… Your Why uses a number or finding from Project 2.

If you're stuck… Start from your strongest Project 2 finding about who was in the ad.

Step 6 of 6

Run the rival test

Check each of your 4 standards. Could Sony or Skullcandy write the same line? Could you check it in a 15-second ad?

Sample (made up)

"Use great music" fails. Any brand could say it. "Music leads the idea" passes. You can see whether the edit follows the track.

You're on track if… All 4 standards pass both questions.

If you're stuck… A standard fails? Add what makes it Beats: the music, the athlete, the real fan.
