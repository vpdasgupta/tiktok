Design Slides People Can Read

Your slides back you up. You do the talking.

  • The slide showsThe point and one piece of proof, big enough to read in a video.
  • You sayThe details, the story and why it matters to Beats.

All numbers in this deck are made up. They come from the example outline in Project 3.

The structure

Use 6–10 slides for 6 minutes

An 8-slide plan (add an Insight 3 slide only if time allows)
SlidesWhat goes on them
1–2Title, then Context: what you studied and why it matters
3–5Your 2–3 insights, each with an action title and its evidence
6–7Your recommendation and an optional creative sample
8One clear next step, then thank you and questions

Text

Make text big and short

  • Titles 36pt or largerOne action title per slide, 5–10 words.
  • Body 24pt or largerIf you can't read it on a phone, it's too small.
  • 3–4 bullets at mostAbout 8–12 words each. No paragraphs.
  • Say it, don't show itPut the full sentences in your speaker notes.

Data

Show one number big

  • One comparison per chartExample (made up): attention 4.5 vs. believable 3.0 for Beats_YouTube_01.
  • Label the barsPut the number on or next to each bar so nobody has to guess.
  • Name the sourceA small line under the chart: "Project 2 survey, 12 responses."
  • Keep it simpleIn Google Slides, use Insert, then Chart, then Bar.

Readable for everyone

Check contrast, color and alt text

  • Strong contrastDark text on a light background, or white text on a dark one.
  • Not color aloneIf red means "fails," also write "Fails."
  • 2–3 colorsUse Beats red as an accent, not for body text.
  • Alt textRight-click an image or chart and choose Alt text. Say what it shows.

Look like Beats

Use your own Beats Creative Standards

Your Framework doc already says what Beats looks like. Use the same rules on your slides.

  • Bold and high-contrastBig type, lots of space, one strong image per slide.
  • Show the adsScreenshots from ads you studied, with the ad ID under each one.
  • Keep it cleanDon't stretch logos or stack effects. Simple fades or no transitions.

Tools

Build in Google Slides. Canva or Gamma are optional.

You submit a Google Slides link, so start and finish there.
ToolUse it for
Google SlidesYour deck. This is the file you share and submit.
CanvaOne custom visual, like storyboard frames. Download it as a PNG and add it to your slides.
GammaA fast first draft from your Presentation Story. Export it as PowerPoint, then in Google Slides use File, then Import slides.

Spend 20 minutes or less on design tools. Your insights matter more than the polish.

Avoid these

Six slide mistakes

  • Too much textPeople read instead of listening.
  • Reading your slidesSay more than what's on screen.
  • Fancy transitionsThey distract. Use none or a simple fade.
  • Too many slidesMore than 10 and you'll rush.
  • Tiny chartsOne comparison, big labels.
  • Mixed stylesSame fonts and colors on every slide.
