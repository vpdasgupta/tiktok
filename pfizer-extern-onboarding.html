Step 4 of 6 · Project overview

Your challenge

Build an AI document intelligence system that reads Pfizer's supply chain files and flags the ones that need a compliance check.

Why Pfizer cares: Pfizer's medicines reach more than 400 million patients a year, and every one needs a paper trail that proves it's safe. Vendor certificates and quality reports can run past 100 pages, and people still check most of them by hand. AI can speed this up, but scanned pages, handwritten signatures, and decades of old formats trip it up.

What you'll do

  1. Weeks 1–3

    Foundations

    1. 01Project 1: Learn how AI reads pharmaceutical documents
      Starts Day 1
    2. 02Project 2: Learn Python for AI document processing
    3. 03Project 3: Extract data from documents with Python

  2. Weeks 4–7

    Search

    1. 04Project 4: Compare OCR engines on complex layouts
    2. 05Project 5: Build a smart search with RAG
    3. 06Project 6: Try advanced RAG and open-source models

  3. Weeks 8–10

    Ship it

    1. 07Project 7: Split, classify, and route document files
    2. 08Project 8: Build a chatbot for document search
    3. 09Project 9: Integrate, test, and present your system

What you'll learn

  • Reading scanned documents with OCR
  • Extracting data with Python in Google Colab
  • Building search with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
  • Classifying and routing documents with AI
  • Building a chatbot interface with Gradio
  • Testing and presenting a working AI system

Help and resources

Getting help

Who do I ask when I'm stuck?

Post in your program's Slack channel or message your program manager. Don't contact Pfizer staff directly; your program manager routes questions to them.

Where do I submit my work?

Each project has a few tasks for you to complete. You'll submit your work on those task pages in the platform.

I missed a live session. What now?

All of your live sessions are recorded and saved in the Recordings tab on your account page.

Is this run by Extern or by Pfizer?

Extern runs your externship: the platform, Slack, scheduling, and support. Pfizer brings the challenge, company mentors, and feedback on your work. You're an Extern, not a Pfizer employee.
