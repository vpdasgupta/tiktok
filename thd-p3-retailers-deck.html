  3. Different job: counting clothes

    Apparel stores use RFID

    Useful for stock and theft, not for changing shelf prices.

    • DecathlonEvery product tagged since 2019. Shrink down 9% (2014). [independent]
    • UniqloCheckout waits roughly halved. [independent]
    • Macy'sShoe display errors ≈ 30% → 4–6% (RFID vendors paid for the study). [independent]

    3 / 5

  4. Check the numbers

    Two claims that didn't hold up

    • Zara's “99% inventory accuracy”No Zara or independent source behind it. Don't use it.
    • “65% → 99% accuracy with RFID”Really 63% → 95%, in apparel stores (Auburn University and GS1 US).

    Most label specs come from the companies selling them. When you use one, write “the vendor claims…”

    4 / 5

  5. Use proof well

    “Walmart does this” isn't a reason

    At the October 7 meeting, Tina, the Home Depot leader who sponsors this program, praised an extern who said, in effect, “Walmart does this, but we may not need it.”

    Ask what the retailer was solving, and whether Home Depot has the same problem.
