Step 3 of 6 · How Extern works

Three parts, one externship

  • Extern

    Runs your externship

  • BeReal

    Brings the challenge

    A real product problem, a company mentor, and feedback on your work in live sessions.

  • You

    Do the work

    Five projects at a suggested pace, plus live sessions with your cohort.

You're an Extern, not a BeReal employee. BeReal brought a real challenge, and your company mentor guides your cohort through it. Extern is your home base.

Step 4 of 6 · Project overview

Your challenge

Help BeReal grow its monthly active users while staying true to its mission of authenticity.

Why BeReal cares: BeReal's promise is no filters and no fakes. People love the idea, but the daily habit is harder to keep. Monthly active users (MAUs) are the people who open the app at least once a month, and BeReal needs ideas that bring more of them back without making the app less real.

What you'll do

  1. Project 1 · Starts Day 1Map the BeReal user journeyGo from sign-up to posting to reacting, and spot friction and delight.
  2. Project 2Benchmark BeReal's competitorsFind which features actually drive growth.
  3. Project 3Design a new app featurePrototype one idea that could grow BeReal's MAUs.
  4. Project 4Test it in focus groupsRun focus groups with peers and refine your feature.
  5. Project 5Pitch your featurePresent a short, data-backed proposal.

What you'll learn

  • Mapping a user journey
  • Benchmarking competitors
  • Prototyping a feature
  • Running focus groups
  • Pitching with data

Help and resources

Getting help

Who do I ask when I'm stuck?

Post in your program's Slack channel or message your program manager. Don't contact BeReal staff directly; your program manager routes questions to them.

Where do I submit my work?

Each project has a few tasks for you to complete. You'll submit your work on those task pages in the platform.

I missed a live session. What now?

All of your live sessions are recorded and saved in the Recordings tab on your account page.

Is this run by Extern or by BeReal?

Extern runs your externship: the platform, Slack, scheduling, and support. BeReal brings the challenge, a mentor, and feedback on your work. You're an Extern, not a BeReal employee.
