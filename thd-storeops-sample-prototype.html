    What to notice

    • Exactly 4 screens are required: 3 for the main flow and 1 for when something goes wrong. The cover doesn't count.
    • The "goes wrong" screen comes from a Guess. Marcus's biggest Guess is that the hdPhone can find an online order, so his goes-wrong screen opens from the small "Cart not found?" button on Screen 1, where the scan happens. It says what went wrong, what to do next, and who can help.
    • Every screen has a caption that starts "What this shows."
    • Rough and gray. Gray boxes, plain words, and orange only on the main button. No logos or photos.
    • Bonus screens are optional. Turn on "Show bonus screens" to see 2 stretch screens: the Pro desk's side of the hand-off and a "done" screen. Only build a bonus once your 4 work end to end.
    • Path B looks like this. If you ask an AI chat to build your prototype as one HTML file, this is about the right amount of polish.
