Your breakeven

Seven numbers move your payback

Each one is a lever: change it and your months change.

  • 1 · Paper tags per smart labelMore is better. Usually 1.
  • 2 · Smart label priceLower is better. $10 in your model.
  • 3 · Seconds per paper changeMore seconds = more labor saved.
  • 4 · Damage rateLower is better. 5% in your model.
  • 5 · Running cost per labelLower is better. $0.50 a year.
  • 6 · Price changes per productMore changes = more labor saved.
  • 7 · Extra labor savedMore is better, if MET does that work.
  • Every idea on this pagemoves one or more of these levers.

Example: ceiling fans

The big idea

Count labels before you polish them

Each fan hangs on display and sits boxed below, so it needs two tags. One label that covers both cuts payback by more than half. That beats any damage fix.

Ideas that change how many labels you need often beat ideas that make each label a little better.
