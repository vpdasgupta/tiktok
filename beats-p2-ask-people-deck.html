Project 2

Two ways to hear from real people

  • A survey gives you numbersEveryone answers the same questions. You get ratings and votes you can count and compare.
  • Comments give you people's own wordsYou read what people already wrote under an ad. You get real quotes, but you can't ask a follow-up.

In this project: your survey is the main source. In Step 3 you can add an optional comment check to find quotes.

Write research questions

Know what your survey can measure

Every survey in this project asks the same core questions about each ad. Write research questions these can answer.

  • Overall ratingHow much did you like this ad? (1–5)
  • Optional commentWhat stood out to you, good or bad? (people's own words)
  • 4 agree or disagree ratings
    • Grabs my attention
    • Easy to remember
    • Believable
    • Makes me want to learn more
  • 3 compare votes
    • Most memorable
    • Most believable
    • Most makes me want to buy

Need something else? You can add 1–2 questions of your own in Step 2.

Write research questions

Test one thing per question

Before and after: one thing per question
WeakStronger
"How do hooks, talent and music affect how memorable an ad is?""Are celebrity-hook ads easier to remember than creator-led ads?"

Why: when three things change at once, you can't tell which one made the difference.

Write research questions

Name the comparison and keep it neutral

Before and after: name the comparison and keep it neutral
ProblemWeakStronger
No comparison"Do people like Beats ads?""Is Beats' creator TikTok more believable than Skullcandy's?"
Leading words"Why are Beats ads better?""How do Beats ads compare with rival ads on believability?"

Neutral means the question doesn't hint at the answer you hope for.

Where this leads

Aim for findings like this

A good finding = number + comparison + so what. Ask questions that can end in one.

Example finding (made up, 12 responses)
Number
Beats_YouTube_01 scored 4.5 of 5 on attention and 3.0 on believable.
Comparison
Highest on attention and lowest on believable of the 4 ads.
So what
Stars get attention, but people may not trust them. Beats could pair the star with a real fan.

Small sample? With fewer than 30 responses, call your findings "early signals."
