How to read this & caveats

  • Programs are defined by job ID (`externship_id`), not title — several were renamed mid-flight. NASCAR, Pfizer, Healthcare Ops are consolidated across their renamed cohorts; Energy Innovation Capital is consolidated across its renamed titles.
  • Cohort scope: programs/cohorts that started in 2026 are included, and each cohort is counted over its full registration window — so January cohorts include sign-ups from December 2025. This keeps per-cohort rates accurate (the denominator matches the whole cohort).
  • Orientation / Project 1 = unique students who completed that module ÷ enrollments. Program completion = a completion certificate issued (`certificate_approved` + `certificate_top_performer`; top performers get that certificate instead of the standard one). This replaces the earlier final-module proxy, which mis-counted where module names were renamed.
  • P1 ≤7d = share of registrants who finished Project 1 within 7 days (7-day funnel). Cancellation = `registration_canceled` ÷ enrollments, excluding students who earned a certificate — so it counts only those who dropped without finishing.
  • Program completion = matured cohorts only. A cohort counts toward completion only once its program has ended plus a 2-week grace window (end = cohort start date + program duration in weeks). In-flight cohorts are shown (greyed, "In-flight" badge) but excluded from the completion number, since they can't have finished yet. "n/total closed" by each program shows how many cohorts are mature; "—" means none have closed. Orientation, Project 1, P1 ≤7d and Cancellation are shown across all cohorts. Note certificates require passing manual review and are issued after a cohort ends, so recently-closed cohorts can read slightly low — treat completion as a conservative floor.
  • Live vs snapshot: Enrolled, Orientation, Project 1, Program and Cancellation refresh live from Mixpanel for 2026 (recomputed across each program's job IDs). P1 ≤7d is a Jun 9 2026 snapshot. Cohort roster is as of Jun 9 2026. Maturity is recomputed against the current date on every open. Mirrors the weekly-refreshed "Externship Program Health" Notion page.
