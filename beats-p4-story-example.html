Final Presentation Outline: Beats Video Ads

Alex Rivera · Projects 1–4

1. Context

I studied 25 ads from Beats and its rivals, then surveyed 12 people about 4 of them. I wanted to find out what makes Beats ads work, and where they don't.

2. Key Insights

  • Stars get attention, not trust. The Beats athlete ad grabbed the most attention (4.5 out of 5) but was the least believable (3.0), and 8 of 12 people said it "felt like an ad."
  • Rivals are catching up with creators. Skullcandy's ads score almost as high as Beats' (4.1 vs 4.2), and 6 of 12 people picked a Skullcandy creator ad as the most believable.

3. Application

  • Beats could pair a star with a real fan in the first 3 seconds, so the ad gets attention and trust. (Insight 1)
  • Next step: test one star-plus-fan TikTok ad against a star-only version.
  • Creative sample: a 3-frame storyboard of the star-plus-fan ad.
