Area 1

Measurement & Planning

You can't recover what you can't measure. The tools that tell DEP how much energy, heat, or material is even there to capture.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Flow & temperature monitoring — non-invasive tech to validate real flow rates and temperatures in sewers (temperature sets the ceiling on recoverable heat).
  • Energy & decarbonization planning tools — calculating carbon impact and running lifecycle-carbon assessments at the design phase.

A strong fit measures or plans for recovery from wastewater or sewers — sewer flow/temperature, or lifecycle-carbon planning tied to water infrastructure. A generic carbon-accounting dashboard with no wastewater tie is Out.

Area 2

Recovery & Conversion

The actual value capture — pulling heat, energy, and materials out of what used to be pure waste.

Named needs DEP called out

  • On-site dewatering — low-cost, low-maintenance drying of wastewater solids.
  • Nutrient recovery — recovering struvite (a phosphorus fertilizer) and volatile fatty acids (chemical feedstocks).
  • Non-invasive sewer heat recovery — capturing heat from sewer flow without shutting the line down.
  • Alternative biogas use — upgrading the methane-rich gas wastewater produces beyond flaring.

A strong fit physically recovers energy, heat, or material from wastewater or sewers. The trap is the opposite of the other challenges: this is the least "sensor on a pipe." Generic clean-energy or recycling with no wastewater tie is Out.

A few terms

Biogas = methane-rich gas from breaking-down solids. Struvite = a recoverable phosphorus fertilizer. VFAs = volatile fatty acids, useful feedstocks. Dewatering = removing water from sludge. Lifecycle carbon = total emissions across a choice's whole life.

Challenge 3: Resource & Energy Recovery

How can DEP capture more value from its wastewater and sewers — recovering energy, heat, and materials? The newest, least-crowded challenge. Two named areas define "relevant." The strong fits are recovery/conversion tech, not sensors.

Area 1 — Measurement & Planning

Non-invasive sewer flow & temperature monitoring; energy and decarbonization planning tools (lifecycle carbon at the design phase).

Area 2 — Recovery & Conversion

On-site dewatering; nutrient recovery (struvite, VFAs); non-invasive sewer heat recovery; alternative biogas use. Terms: biogas = methane-rich gas from solids; struvite = a phosphorus fertilizer; VFAs = volatile fatty acids; dewatering = removing water from sludge; lifecycle carbon = total emissions across a choice's whole life.

Relevance check — answer key

Real fits: struvite recovery (Area 2); sewer heat recovery (Area 2); non-invasive sewer flow/temperature measurement (Area 1); biogas upgrading (Area 2). Not fits: rooftop solar (clean energy, no wastewater tie); a recycling-bin route optimizer (recycling, not sewer recovery); a corporate ESG carbon dashboard (accounting, not recovery).
