The Home Depot · Project 2 · Store A

Breakeven Builder

    1. Pick your store area

    Your program manager emailed you your area. Pick it, and Store A's map shows where it is. You'll work out its numbers on step 2.

    Store A map

    2. Work out your four estimates

    Work out each estimate for your area. Each one says where to find what you need and gives a hint for your area to get you started. Then say why in one line: your reasons become your Assumptions Log.

    Changing a number clears your checked calculations on step 3, so you can redo them with the new number.

    3. Do the four calculations

    Work each one out with a calculator, type your answer, and press Check. Round dollars to the nearest dollar and months to the nearest month. For calculation 4, type never if the running cost is bigger than the labor saved.

    4. Read your damage test

    The Builder reruns calculation 4 at four damage rates. Only the running cost changes.

    Finish all four calculations on step 3 to see your damage test.
    Type a much higher or lower number to see how the damage test moves. This doesn't change your saved estimate.

    One sentence.
    Use the box above, then write one sentence.

    5. Fill in the store map

    Add your result to the shared map of Store A. Once you do, you'll see every other extern's result too. Then compare an area that pays back with one that doesn't.

    Example: "Fan prices change about 10 times a year, so payback takes about 8 years: too slow."

    Everyone's map of Store A

    Each area is labeled with its middle result and how many externs modeled it. The table below the map gives the same information.

      6. Download your model as a PDF

      Your PDF is the model part of your Breakeven Analysis. It holds your estimates and reasons, your four calculations, your damage test, your comparison and your map.

      Before you download

        In the print window, choose Save as PDF. Nothing happens? Open the Builder in a new window; your work comes with you.
