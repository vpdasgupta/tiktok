How to Record a Video Presentation

Record 6 minutes of you and your slides

  • What leadership seesYour slides full screen, with your face in a small camera bubble.
  • How long6 minutes or less. Shorter and clear beats longer and rushed.

The practice times in this deck are made up.

Your audience

Talk to Beats leadership

  • They know BeatsSkip the brand history. Start with what you studied.
  • They're busySay your answer in the first minute.
  • They want a next stepEnd with one thing Beats could try.
  • They'll ask questionsKnow where every number came from.

Practice

Rehearse out loud from short notes

  • Use your speaker notesShort cue lines, not a script to read word for word.
  • Time every runUse your phone's timer. Write the time down.
  • Practice with someoneA friend, a family member or a classmate. Ask what they remember.
  • Try a speaking coachYoodli is an AI tool that gives feedback on pace and filler words.

Tools

Pick a tool that gives you a link

Any of these work. Loom is the simplest.
ToolHow to record
LoomFree account. Choose Screen + Camera, then start recording. You get a link when you finish.
ZoomStart a meeting by yourself, share your slides and click Record.
Google MeetPresent your slides and record, if your account allows recording.

Set up

Get set up in 5 minutes

  • Quiet roomClose the door. Turn off notifications.
  • Light in front of youA window or lamp behind the camera, not behind you.
  • Camera at eye levelStack books under your laptop if you need to.
  • Test 10 secondsRecord a short clip and play it back before the real take.

Delivery

Talk to one person through the camera

  • Look at the lensGlance at your notes, then back to the camera.
  • Slow downPause between Context, Insights and Application.
  • Say numbers simply"4.5 out of 5" is easier to hear than "four point five on a five-point scale."
  • Keep goingStumbled on a word? Pause and say it again. You don't need to restart.
