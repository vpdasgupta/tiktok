Optional: Quick Comment Check

Check real comments on 2 of your ads

Optional

You'll log 15–20 top comments on a Comments tab in your response Sheet. Comments are what people say when nobody asked them, so they're a good check on your survey.

  • Worth doing if…Your ads have at least 10 public comments each, or your survey came in small.
  • Skip it if…Comments are off or there are only a few. You'll mark your findings "One source."

Skipping won't count against you. All sample comments in this deck are made up.

Step 1 of 5

Pick 2 ads

Choose 2 of your 4 tested ads that have active comments. A good pair is the Beats ad with your most surprising result and the rival ad that scored most differently.

Sample (made up)
Beats_YouTube_01
Top attention score (4.5) but the lowest believable score (3.0)
Skullcandy_TikTok_01
Highest believable score (4.2), the biggest gap from Beats

You're on track if… Both ads are from your survey, and each has at least 10 comments.

If you're stuck… Comments are off on one ad? Swap in another tested ad, or skip this task.

Step 2 of 5

Set up your Comments tab

In your response Sheet, add a tab named Comments with 4 headers in row 1.

You're on track if… The tab sits in the same Sheet as your Clean and Results tabs.

If you're stuck… Don't start a new file. Click + at the bottom of your response Sheet.

Step 3 of 5

Copy the top comments

  1. Open each ad and sort by top comments. On YouTube, choose Sort by > Top comments. TikTok and Instagram show top comments first.
  2. Take 15–20 comments in total, about 8–10 per ad, in the order they appear.
  3. Skip only spam, emoji-only and off-topic comments.
  4. For long comments, copy only the key sentence.

You're on track if… You have 15–20 rows, in the order they showed up.

If you're stuck… A comment is all emoji? Skip it and take the next one.

Step 4 of 5

Code each comment

Sentiment
Positive, Negative, Mixed or Neutral. If you have to squint to see an opinion, it's Neutral.
Theme
Reuse your codes from the Build Your Results Tab task, like Feels like an ad or Trusts the person. Add a new theme only when none fits.
Sample (made up)
"Another ad pretending to be a vibe."
Negative · Feels like an ad
"Ok now I need these for the gym."
Positive · Wants to try it

You're on track if… Every row has one sentiment and one theme.

If you're stuck… Can't tell the sentiment? Call it Neutral and move on.

Step 5 of 5

Write a count

Under the table, write 1–2 lines with a count you can use in Step 4.

Sample (made up)

I logged 20 comments, 10 on Beats_YouTube_01 and 10 on Skullcandy_TikTok_01. On Beats_YouTube_01, 6 of 10 were about the athlete, not the headphones.

You're on track if… Your line gives a number out of a total for at least 1 ad.

If you're stuck… No clear pattern? Count your most common theme anyway.

Follow these rules

Study comments, don't join them

  • No usernames.Copy the words only. Never note who wrote a comment.
  • Don't engage.Don't like, reply to or report the comments you're studying.
  • Don't cherry-pick.Take the top comments in order, good and bad. Don't hunt for ones that agree with your survey.

Next up: In Step 4, you'll use these comments to mark findings "Backed up" or "Challenged."
