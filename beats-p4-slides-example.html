Slide 6 of 8

Application

Pair a star with a real fan in the first 3 seconds

  • From Insight 1: the star brings attention, the fan brings trust.
  • Principle #1: Show a real person in the first 3 seconds.
  • Principle #2: Let the music lead. It's where Beats still stands apart.

Source: Strategic Recommendations Summary in my Framework doc

Speaker notes

So Beats could pair a star with a real fan in the first 3 seconds. The star gets people to look. The fan gives them a reason to believe. (0:45)
