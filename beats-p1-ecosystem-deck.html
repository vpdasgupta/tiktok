Why this project

Most video ads get skipped. A few break through.

Every day, over 3 billion people open TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. Millions of ads are served, skipped and forgotten every minute.

When Beats launched the Beats Fit Pro with Kim Kardashian in 2022, it faced the same skip buttons and the same 3-second window as everyone else. The campaign still drew millions of views and drove strong sales.

The big idea: video advertising is an ecosystem, not a channel. The ads that win understand the system they run in.

Then vs. now

Creative first is not enough anymore

The old model: creative first

Make a great ad, buy media time, hope people watch. It worked when there were three TV channels and no skip button.

The new reality: systems first

Your ad has to get past platform rules, an algorithm, a viewer's split-second judgment and every competitor in the same feed.

Miss any one of these and even brilliant creative fails.

The four forces

Four forces shape every video ad

  • 1 · Platform formatsEach platform has its own shape, sound default and hook window
  • 2 · Algorithm logicMachine learning decides who sees your ad, and it rewards watch time
  • 3 · Attention economicsViewers decide in a split second whether an ad earns their time
  • 4 · Competitive contextYour ad sits next to AirPods, Sony, Bose and JBL

You'll go deeper on forces 1–3 in 1.3 How Video Ads Win Attention and on force 4 in 1.4 Meet the Competition.

Compound effects

The forces work together

Platform + algorithm
TikTok's algorithm favors native content, so brands make ads that don't look like ads
Algorithm + attention
Early drop-off tells the algorithm the ad is weak, so it gets shown less
Attention + competition
When every competitor uses celebrity hooks, viewers tune them out
Competition + platform
Gaps on one platform are openings, such as no premium brand owning creator content
