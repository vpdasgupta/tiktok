Real example Biometric privacy

Facebook knew your friends’ faces.

For years, uploading a photo triggered “Tag Suggestions.” It scanned the faces and guessed who was who, building a faceprint: a unique map of your face, a lot like a fingerprint.

📸 A faceprint is a body-based ID you can’t change

Real example One state had a rule

Illinois said: ask first.

Illinois’ Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA) says a company must get your written permission before scanning a face or fingerprint. A group of Illinois users sued, arguing Facebook never asked.

📌 BIPA = get written consent before you scan

Real example The outcome

$650M

One state’s rule, real teeth.

In 2021 a judge approved a $650 million settlement, about $345 each to 1.6 million Illinoisans. That is why BIPA is the law most people map first in this lane.
