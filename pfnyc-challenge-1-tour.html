Use case 1

Extreme Heat & Crew Safety

Keeping field crews safe and operational in dangerous heat. Yes — physical worker-safety and cooling gear counts here, not just software.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Thermally regulated workwear — materials that help field workers stay cool on the job.
  • Alternative cooling & mobile water-dispensing — low-staff mobile units deployable for water-main breaks or community events.
  • Hydrant locking & adaptive cooling retrofits — uniform locking and cooling retrofits for the city's ~90,000 fire hydrants.

A strong fit protects DEP's crews or keeps field work running in heat — cooling gear, workwear, mobile water. The trap is dismissing hardware as "not tech." If it keeps a crew safe or operational, it's in.

Use case 2

Field Operations & Imagery

Better tools to capture what's happening on the ground — in the field, not from a desk.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Field-based data-collection tools — document and assess construction progress or project risk in the field.
  • Ground-level street imagery — frequently updated, sidewalk-level visuals verifying the condition of green-infrastructure assets and street furnishings.

A strong fit helps DEP record or see field conditions faster — imagery, mobile capture, progress tracking. A generic photo app with no tie to field assets or construction is not.

Use case 3

Cybersecurity (OT/SCADA + AI Governance)

Two flavors of security — protecting the systems that physically run the utility, plus governing how staff use AI.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Cybersecurity for OT & SCADA networks — protecting the systems that run pumps and valves from cyber threats.
  • AI usage monitoring, governance & risk — understanding how DEP staff use AI, including adoption insight and risk.

OT/SCADA-for-water cyber is In — it feels "not water-y," but it protects the machinery that moves the water, so it's a High fit. IT-only cyber that just guards office laptops and email is Out. Named beats intuition every time.

Use case 4

Modelling

Predicting demand, flow, and storm risk so DEP can size and plan ahead of the weather.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Water-demand & wastewater-flow modeling — AI tools projecting demand in real time for infrastructure sizing.
  • Advanced weather modeling for stormwater risk — spatial/temporal precipitation forecasting to find localized flood-risk hotspots.

A strong fit predicts water, flow, or storm behavior for DEP's planning. A consumer weather app for planning your weekend is not — the need is city-scale flood-risk and demand modeling.

Use case 5

Capital Planning & Data

The software backbone of planning — costs, filings, road-maps, metrics, and complaints.

Named needs DEP called out

  • Materials cost tracking & estimation — real-time materials cost and supply-chain risk modeling.
  • Automated triage for chemical-safety filings — auto-reviewing annual Community Right-to-Know reports.
  • Strategic planning & road-mapping — flag spatial/timing conflicts across maintenance, testing, and capital projects.
  • Cross-team KPI tracking & inquiry / complaint tracking — fewer duplicate complaints and dropped tickets.

A strong fit is software tied to utility planning, cost, filings, or operations. The trap: a generic HR or scheduling app for any office looks like "planning software" but has no utility tie — that's Out.

Challenge 1: Planning, Data & Field Operations

How can DEP make planning, cost estimation, field data collection, and administrative review faster and more data-driven? Five use cases define what "relevant" means. Match a company to any one of them and it can be a High fit.

Use case 1 — Extreme Heat & Crew Safety

Thermally regulated workwear; alternative cooling & mobile water-dispensing; hydrant locking & cooling retrofits. Physical safety gear counts.

Use case 2 — Field Operations & Imagery

Field-based data-collection tools; ground-level street imagery verifying green-infrastructure condition.

Use case 3 — Cybersecurity (OT/SCADA + AI Governance)

OT/SCADA cyber for the systems that run pumps and valves (In — even though it feels "not water-y"); AI usage monitoring and governance. IT-only office cyber is Out.

Use case 4 — Modelling

Water-demand & wastewater-flow modeling; advanced weather modeling for stormwater flood risk.

Use case 5 — Capital Planning & Data

Materials cost estimation; automated triage for chemical-safety filings; strategic road-mapping; KPI and complaint tracking.

Relevance check — answer key

Real fits: real-time water-demand modeling (Use case 4); chemical-safety filing triage (Use case 5); OT/SCADA cyber (Use case 3); thermally regulated workwear (Use case 1); sidewalk-level street imagery (Use case 2). Not fits: office-laptop antivirus (IT-only cyber is out); a generic HR scheduling app (no utility tie); a consumer weekend-weather app (not city-scale flood modeling).
