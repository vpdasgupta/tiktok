What Makes an Ad Feel Like Beats

Recognizable before the logo

  • VoiceHow the ad talks: the words on screen and out loud.
  • LookColor, energy and how the product shows up.
  • SoundThe music and how the edit follows it.
  • TalentWho's in the ad and what they make people believe.

Creative standards are the rules that make an ad feel like Beats and not Sony or Skullcandy. You'll write one Do and one Don't for each area.

Voice

Confident, short, direct

Made-up lines to compare
Sounds like BeatsDoesn't
"Turn it up.""Elevate your listening experience today."
"Built for game day.""40-hour battery, 3 noise modes and fast charging."
"Your sound. Your rules.""Maybe these headphones are for you."

Quick test: would you say it out loud to a friend? If it sounds like a spec sheet, rewrite it.

Look

Bold, high-contrast, product in motion

What to look for in the first few seconds
More BeatsLess Beats
Headphones worn and moving: a run, a warm-up, a studioHeadphones spinning on a white background
Bold color and strong contrastSoft, muted, faded color
Cuts that land on the beatCuts that ignore the music

Links to your playbook: a hook in the first 3 seconds needs movement. A still product shot gives people nothing to stop for.

Sound

Music leads the idea

Music first
The track shapes the edit. It isn't added at the end.
Pick on purpose
The genre tells people who the ad is for.
Trending or original
Trending sounds fit fast TikTok posts. An original track can become something only Beats has.
Sound off
Captions and text still carry the message when the sound is off.

Beats sells sound. If the music could be swapped for any stock track, the ad isn't using its biggest strength.

Talent

Who's in the ad changes what people believe

  • StarGets attention fast. Can feel like an ad.
  • CreatorFeels honest and native to the platform.
  • Real fanRelatable. "Someone like me uses these."
Example (made up)

In the Project 2 example survey, Beats_YouTube_01 (star-led) scored 4.5 on attention but 3.0 on believable. Skullcandy_TikTok_01 (creator-led) scored 4.2 on believable.

The format

Each standard = 1 Do + 1 Don't + Why

Do
Pair stars with real fans or creators.
Don't
No star-only ad with no real voice.
Why
Stars get attention, not trust. Beats_YouTube_01 scored 4.5 on attention but 3.0 on believable (made up).

A good Don't names something you could spot in an ad. "Don't be boring" isn't checkable. "No static packshot opener" is.

Before you keep a standard

Run the rival test

  • Could a rival say it?If Sony or Skullcandy could use the same line, make it more Beats.
  • Can you check it?Watch a 15-second ad. Can you say yes or no?
  • Is it from your research?Tie it to your playbook, your Ad Repository or your survey.
Example (made up)

"Use great music" fails. Any brand could say it. "Music leads the idea" passes. You can see whether the edit follows the track.
