Challenge & Lane

The problem, in my words

DEP learns a pipe or asset has failed only after it breaks. Challenge 2 is an early-warning system for the condition of pipes, equipment, and the water environment.

Why it matters to DEP

An undetected leak wastes treated water and risks a main break under a city street — early detection makes DEP predictive, not reactive.

My lane — and why I chose it

Challenge 2 → Water supply & environmental field monitoring. Low-cost water-level sensors and rain gauges → DEP's "cloudburst / GI monitoring" need. Chosen because it's verifiable: a sensor has real deployments, or it doesn't.
