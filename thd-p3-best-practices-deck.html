  1. Best practice 1 of 6

    Start where paper is slowest

    Locked cases, cages, high shelves

    Lever 3

    Does
    Puts smart labels first where a paper change means find a key, unlock, swap, relock.
    Price
    None. It's a placement choice.
    Proof
    Home Depot already uses Chroma in the front-of-store locked case.
    Watch out
    You need a believable time per change. Ask MET, or label it an estimate.

  2. Best practice 2 of 6

    Buy the cheapest size that fits

    Small black-and-white where you can

    Lever 2

    Does
    Uses a small label wherever the shelf doesn't need a bigger screen.
    Price
    Small: about $5–$9, vs. $11–$17 for 4.2 in. [vendor]
    Watch out
    The price must stay readable. Unit-price rules may set a minimum text size.

    2 / 6

  3. Best practice 3 of 6

    Batch updates; swap batteries

    Overnight updates, replaceable batteries

    Lever 5

    Does
    Sends price changes in one batch so batteries last, and swaps batteries, not labels.
    Price
    None for batching. A battery costs less than a label.
    Proof
    Chroma batteries: up to 10 years at 2–3 updates a day [vendor]
    Watch out
    Areas repriced daily drain batteries faster.

    3 / 6

  4. Best practice 4 of 6

    Put replacements in the contract

    Broken labels and batteries covered

    Levers 4 + 5

    Does
    Builds replacement labels and battery swaps into the yearly software fee.
    Price
    Part of the software fee. No published price.
    Proof
    Some licenses cover “replacement labels, battery replacement” [vendor]
    Watch out
    It only counts if it's in writing.

    4 / 6

  5. Best practice 5 of 6

    Check the signal first

    Before any label goes up

    Keeps months real

    Does
    Tests coverage in far corners and adds a gateway where the signal is weak.
    Price
    A one-time survey, plus gateways where needed.
    Why
    The last whole-store test ended mostly because remote labels couldn't update.
    Watch out
    This is also your top risk fix on page 4.

    5 / 6

  6. Best practice 6 of 6

    Reprice where markets move

    Lumber, copper, online competitors

    Lever 6

    Does
    Follows market prices more closely, so each label does more changes.
    Price
    None.
    Proof
    Stores with e-labels made more, smaller price changes [independent]
    Watch out
    Walmart rules out surge pricing; some states regulate it. More updates drain batteries.
