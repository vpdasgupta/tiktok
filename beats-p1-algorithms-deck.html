How Algorithms Decide

Why some ads reach millions and others don't · 6 slides · use Next or swipe

Gatekeepers

Algorithms decide who sees your ad

You could make a brilliant ad: perfect hook, stunning visuals, music that slaps. If the algorithm doesn't show it to the right people at the right time, it might as well not exist.

Your ad isn't competing for shelf space. It's competing with every other video in the feed for seconds of attention.

Every algorithm solves the same problem: keep people on the platform by showing them content they'll engage with.

Engagement signals

What algorithms measure

Watch time
How long people watch before scrolling. Signals quality and relevance.
Engagement
Likes, comments, shares and saves. Shows active interest.
Completion rate
The share of people who watch to the end. Signals compelling content.
Click-through
Taps on the profile, links or call to action. Shows intent to act.
Negative signals
Skips, hides and reports. Tell the algorithm to limit reach.

TikTok

The engagement machine

Follower count matters less than how the content performs, which is why unknown creators can go viral.

Very high
Watch time and completion: grab people immediately and don't lose them
High
Replays and shares: worth watching again, worth sending to a friend
Medium
Comments and profile visits: conversation and curiosity about the brand

TikTok rewards content that keeps people watching, not just content that gets likes.

Instagram

Several algorithms, one app

Instagram doesn't have one algorithm. Each surface ranks content its own way.

Feed and Stories

Relationship (how often you engage with the account), your interests, how recent the post is and how long you've been browsing.

Reels (closer to TikTok)

Entertainment value, inspiration, trending audio and production quality: clear visuals and good lighting.

Familiarity helps in Feed and Stories. In Reels, the content has to earn the watch on its own.

YouTube

The watch-time king

  • Watch timeTotal minutes people spend watching
  • Click-through rateThe share of impressions that become views
  • Average view durationHow long viewers stay before leaving
  • EngagementLikes, comments and subscribes

Ads with low skip rates get better placements at lower cost. That's why the first 5 seconds are worth more than the rest of the ad combined.

So what?

What this means for creative

TikTok
Prioritizes completion and replays. Hook instantly, keep the momentum and give people a reason to rewatch.
Instagram
Prioritizes relationship and engagement. Build brand familiarity and give people something to save and share.
YouTube
Prioritizes watch time and click-through. Nail the first 5 seconds and deliver what the thumbnail promised.
