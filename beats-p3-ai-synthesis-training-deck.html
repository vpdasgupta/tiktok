Using AI to Pull Your Framework Together

AI finds patterns fast. You decide what's true for Beats.

  • AI doesReads your 3 sections at once, spots ideas that repeat and drafts options for you to react to.
  • You doCheck every claim against your doc, pick what matters most for Beats and write the final words.

All examples in this deck are made up.

The goal

What a strong principle looks like

Specific
It says what to do.
Actionable
A creative team could try it next week.
Evidence-based
You can point to a score, a finding or a rule in your doc.
Beats-specific
It would sound wrong coming from Sony.
Memorable
You can say it without notes.

The test

Could anyone say this?

Example (made up): weak principles and stronger versions
Weak: any brand could say itStronger: only Beats would say it
"Start with a good hook.""Show a real person in the first 3 seconds."
"Use music well.""Let the music lead."
"Explain the features.""Put one product fact inside a story."

The prompt

Ask for patterns, not a finished summary

Sample prompt

Here are 3 sections of my Creative Strategy Framework for Beats by Dre, plus my 3 survey findings. Find 5 patterns that show up in at least 2 of them. For each one, quote the lines that support it. Use only my text. Don't add facts, numbers or sources that aren't in it.

Free versions of Claude or ChatGPT work for this. Take your name and any private links out before you paste.

Check it

Check everything that comes back

  • Is every number in my doc?AI can make up numbers and sources. Cut anything you can't find.
  • Could any brand say it?If yes, make it specific or cut it.
  • Is it just one rule repeated?A principle should pull from 2 or more sections.
  • Does it clash with your evidence?Your evidence wins.

Decide

Keep 3, cut the rest

You'll get more patterns than you need. Keep the 3 with the strongest evidence and the clearest link to Beats.

  • KeepShows up in 2 or more sections and has a number behind it.
  • CutOnly in one section, or only in AI's answer.

Your summary should sound like you. Rewrite AI's wording in your own words.

Be open

Say how you used AI

Add one line at the bottom of your summary: what you asked AI to do and what you changed.

Example (made up)

How I used AI: I asked Claude for patterns across Sections 1–3. I kept 2 of its 5 ideas, rewrote both and wrote principle #3 myself.
