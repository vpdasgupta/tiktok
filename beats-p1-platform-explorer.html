TikTok

The native content playground

TikTok ads don't look like ads. They feel personal, and that's the point.

Format
Vertical 9:16

On by default

Key ad formats

  • In-Feed AdsAppear between organic videos in the For You feedBest for: Clicks, conversions, app installs
  • TopViewThe first video people see when they open the appBest for: Maximum awareness, product launches
  • Branded MissionThe brand invites creators to make videos for a campaign, then boosts the best ones as adsBest for: Community engagement, creator-made content
  • Spark AdsBoosted organic posts from the brand or a creatorBest for: An authentic feel, reusing content that already works

Golden rules

  1. Vertical only. 9:16, full screen.
  2. Sound on. Music and audio are central to the experience.
  3. The first 1–3 seconds matter most. People scroll past in milliseconds.
  4. Native wins. Ads that look like TikToks beat polished commercials.

For Beats: make it feel like something a friend posted, not a commercial.

Instagram

The visual storyteller

Instagram ads live across several surfaces, and each has its own rules.

Format

Key ad formats

  • Feed AdsSquare 1:1, portrait 4:5 or landscape 16:9Best for: Detailed storytelling, product showcases
  • Stories AdsVertical 9:16, full screen between StoriesBest for: Urgency, time-sensitive offers
  • Reels AdsVertical 9:16, between ReelsBest for: Entertainment-first content, younger audiences
  • Explore AdsAppear as people browse ExploreBest for: Discovery, reaching new audiences

Golden rules

  1. Visual quality matters. Higher production value than TikTok.
  2. Assume sound off. Many people scroll without audio, so captions are essential.
  3. The first 3 seconds are critical. The hook has to work visually, with no sound.
  4. Stories feel temporary. Casual and behind-the-scenes content fits.

For Beats: the ad has to look great and make sense on mute.

YouTube

The long-form arena

YouTube is where brands tell complete stories, but viewers are ready to skip.

Format
Landscape 16:9
Sound
On, expected
Hook window
5 seconds, before skip

Key ad formats

  • Skippable in-streamNo set maximum (Google suggests under 3 minutes), with a Skip button after 5 secondsBest for: Storytelling, tutorials, longer content
  • Non-skippable in-streamUsually 15 seconds (up to 60 on some TV campaigns), no skipBest for: A guaranteed full view, broad awareness
  • Bumper Ads6 seconds max, no skipBest for: Reinforcement, brand recall
  • MastheadTop of the YouTube home feedBest for: Massive reach, product launches

Golden rules

  1. The first 5 seconds are everything. Hook before the Skip button appears.
  2. Assume sound on. Music, voice-over and dialogue are expected.
  3. Landscape leads. 16:9 is standard, though Shorts are changing this.
  4. Story arcs are welcome. Viewers accept longer narratives.

For Beats: earn the next 25 seconds in the first 5.
