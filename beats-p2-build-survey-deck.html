2.2 Build and Test Your Survey

Build your survey in Google Forms

The question list is ready. Your job is to build it, add your 4 ads and test it.

  • A Google Form4 ads, the same questions for each, 3 compare questions
  • A response SheetWhere answers land. You analyze it in Step 3.
  • A tested survey2 people try it before anyone else sees it

Have open: the P2 Survey Plan tab in your Ad Repository.

Step 1 of 7

Start a new Form and set it up

  1. Go to forms.google.com and click Blank form.
  2. Name it Headphone Ad Survey, [Your Name]. Leave brand names out so you don't sway people.
  3. Open the Settings tab at the top. Under Responses, set Collect email addresses to Do not collect.
  4. Under Presentation, turn on Show progress bar. Set the confirmation message to "Thanks! Your answers help a student ad project."

You're on track if… Your form has a neutral title and doesn't collect emails.

If you're stuck… Can't find Settings? Look for the Questions, Responses and Settings tabs at the top of the form.

Step 2 of 7

Copy the question list

Build sections 1–4 from this list. Add a section with the Add section icon in the floating toolbar.

Question list to copy

SECTION 1 · Welcome Form description: This anonymous survey takes about 8 minutes. It's for a student project on video ads. You'll watch 4 short ads and answer a few questions about each. Q: Are you 18 or older? Multiple choice: Yes / No. Required. SECTION 2 · Ad 1 Section description: Please watch the whole ad, then answer. Q: How much did you like this ad? Linear scale 1–5. 1 = Not at all, 5 = A lot. Required. Q: How much do you agree? Multiple choice grid. Require a response in each row. Rows: Grabs my attention / Easy to remember / Believable / Makes me want to learn more Columns: Strongly disagree / Disagree / Neither agree nor disagree / Agree / Strongly agree Q: What stood out to you, good or bad? Paragraph. Not required. SECTION 3 · Compare Section description: Think about all 4 ads you just watched. Q: Which ad was the most memorable? Q: Which was the most believable? Q: Which would most make you want to buy? Each one: Multiple choice. Options: Ad 1 / Ad 2 / Ad 3 / Ad 4. Required. SECTION 4 · About you Q: What is your age range? Multiple choice: 18–24 / 25–34 / 35+ Q: Which platform do you use most for videos? Multiple choice: TikTok / Instagram / YouTube / Other

Last, set the screener. On the 18+ question, click ⋮ > Go to section based on answer. Set No to Submit form.

You're on track if… Your form has 4 sections in this order: Welcome, Ad 1, Compare, About you.

If you're stuck… Lost your place? The same list is in the text version under the slides.

Step 3 of 7

Add your first ad

  • YouTube linkIn the Ad 1 section, click Add video in the floating toolbar. Paste the YouTube link and click Select. The video plays inside the form.
  • TikTok or Instagram linkIn the Ad 1 description, write "Watch the ad here, then come back:" and paste the link. Add a required question, "Did you watch the whole ad?", with one option: Yes, I watched it.

You're on track if… The Ad 1 title says only "Ad 1." The brand shows up in the ad, not in the title.

If you're stuck… Video won't add? Some videos block embedding. Use the link method instead.

Step 4 of 7

Copy the section for Ads 2, 3 and 4

  1. At the top of the Ad 1 section, click ⋮ > Duplicate section. Do this 3 times.
  2. Rename the copies Ad 2, Ad 3 and Ad 4, in order.
  3. In each copy, swap in the next ad from your P2 Survey Plan tab.
  4. In Ad 3's grid only, add a fifth row: For this row, pick Agree.

You're on track if… Ads 1–4 sit between Welcome and Compare, each with its own ad.

If you're stuck… A copy landed in the wrong place? Click ⋮ > Move section.

Step 5 of 7

Add 1–2 of your own questions

Look at your 3 research questions. If the core questions can't answer one of them, add a question to the Compare section. Skip this if they're all covered.

Sample (made up)
Research question
Do celebrity hooks make Beats ads more believable, or just more noticeable?
My extra question
"Did you recognize the person in Ad 1?" Yes / No / Not sure

You're on track if… Each extra question matches one research question and uses neutral words.

If you're stuck… Not sure an extra question is neutral? Run it through the AI bias-check prompt from 2.1.

Step 6 of 7

Send responses to a Google Sheet

  1. Open the Responses tab and click Link to Sheets.
  2. Choose Create a new spreadsheet. Name it Headphone Ad Survey Responses, [Your Name].
  3. Open the Sheet. Click Share and set General access to Anyone with the link, Viewer.

You're on track if… Your Sheet has one column per question in row 1.

If you're stuck… In Steps 3 and 4 you'll add Results and Findings tabs to this same Sheet.

Step 7 of 7

Test it, fix it, then clear the test answers

  1. Click Send, copy the link and open it on your phone. Check each video plays and each grid fits the screen.
  2. Ask 2 people to take it. Ask them: How long did it take? Was any question confusing? Did every ad play?
  3. Fix what they found. Write down one thing you changed.
  4. In the Responses tab, click ⋮ > Delete all responses. Then delete the test rows in your Sheet too. Keep row 1.

You're on track if… Your survey takes about 8 minutes, and both the Form and the Sheet show 0 responses.

If you're stuck… Grid too wide on a phone? Shorten the row labels. Taking too long? Drop your extra questions first.

How it will be evaluated

What a strong survey looks like

Same core questions
All 4 ads use the same block. The compare and About you questions are there too.
Quality checks
An 18+ screener that ends the form, and one attention-check row
Ads that play
Each ad plays in the form or opens from a link with a "Yes, I watched it" check
Tested
2 testers, one change you made, and no test answers left in the Form or the Sheet
