Build and Test Your Rubric

Add Section 3 to your Framework doc: Creative Rubric

All samples in this deck are made up.

Step 1 of 7

Name your 5 criteria

  1. Under the heading 3. Creative Rubric, add a table with 4 columns: Criterion, 1, 3, 5.
  2. Reread Sections 1 and 2. Pick 5 things every Beats ad should get right.
  3. Give each a short name and one question.
Sample (made up)

Hook · Brand · Feels real · Sound leads · Platform fit

You're on track if… Each criterion traces back to a rule or standard in your doc.

If you're stuck… Start with your 3 universal rules from the Playbook, then add 2 from your Standards.

Step 2 of 7

Write a 1, a 3 and a 5 for each one

Describe what you'd see or hear. 2 and 4 mean "in between."

Sample (made up)
Feels real: 1
No person, or a voiceover that sounds like an ad.
Feels real: 3
A person is there but sounds scripted.
Feels real: 5
A real fan or creator speaks in their own words.

You're on track if… Someone else could score an ad using only your words.

If you're stuck… Write the 5 first, then its opposite as the 1. The 3 sits in between.

Step 3 of 7

Set your pass rule

One line under your table: a total to reach and a floor no criterion can drop below.

Sample (made up)

Pass = 18 or more out of 25, and no criterion below 3.

You're on track if… You can say in one sentence why each number is there.

If you're stuck… Use the sample rule. It works for most rubrics.

Step 4 of 7

Pick your 4 test ads

2 Beats ads and 2 rival ads from your Ad Repository. If you can, use the 4 ads from your Project 2 survey so you can compare with what people said.

Sample (made up)

Beats_YouTube_01 · Beats_TikTok_01 · Skullcandy_TikTok_01 · Sony_YouTube_01

You're on track if… You expect at least one ad to pass and one to fail.

If you're stuck… An ad was taken down? Swap in another from the same brand and platform.

Step 5 of 7

Watch, score and apply your pass rule

Key: YT = YouTube_01, TT = TikTok_01, Skull = Skullcandy. Real = Feels real, Sound = Sound leads, Fit = Platform fit.

Made up, out of 25
AdHookBrandRealSoundFitTotal
Beats YT5425319 Fail
Beats TT4344520 Pass
Skull TT4353520 Pass
Sony YT2542316 Fail

You're on track if… You watched each ad twice, and each score of 2 or lower has a one-line reason.

If you're stuck… Torn between 2 numbers? Pick the closer definition.

Step 6 of 7

Check the scores against your evidence

Do your scores agree with what you found in Projects 1 and 2? Write one line per ad.

Sample (made up)
Beats_YouTube_01
Fails on Feels real (2). Matches Project 2: lowest on believable (3.0), and 8 of 12 said it felt like an ad.
Sony_YouTube_01
Hook is a 2. Matches Project 2: least memorable of the 4 (3.1).

You're on track if… You have one line per ad, and each names a number from Project 1 or 2.

If you're stuck… A score doesn't match? Check the wording. If it was unclear, fix it, score again and note what changed.

Step 7 of 7

Write "Where Beats can stand apart"

Start from the threat and opening on your Positioning tab from Project 1. Then use your scores to answer 3 questions.

Sample (made up)
Where Beats leads
Sound leads: 5 and 4 for Beats vs. 3 and 2 for rivals.
Where rivals match or beat Beats
Feels real: Skullcandy_TikTok_01 got a 5. Beats got a 2 and a 4.
The opening
Sound-led ads with real fans.

You're on track if… Each line names a criterion and the scores behind it.

If you're stuck… List each criterion with Beats scores on one side and rival scores on the other. Look for the biggest gap.

How it will be evaluated

What a strong Creative Rubric section looks like

5 criteria
Each comes from your Playbook or Standards and asks one question
Definitions and pass rule
1 / 3 / 5 describe what you'd see or hear; the rule has a total and a floor
Test scores
2 Beats and 2 rival ads; totals add up; low scores explained
Evidence check
One line per ad, tied to Project 1 or 2
Where Beats can stand apart
Names criteria and scores; ends with one opening
