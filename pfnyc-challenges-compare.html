DEP's 2026 challenges

Three challenges — you'll own one for the whole program. Meet each one.

1

Planning, Data & Field Operations

The challengeHow can DEP make planning, cost estimation, field data collection, and administrative review faster and more data-driven?

This is the office-and-fieldwork side of running a giant water utility — making plans, pricing out projects, gathering data out in the field, keeping crews safe in extreme heat, and getting through mountains of review and paperwork. The goal is to make all of that quicker and better informed by data.

The five areas inside it

Extreme Heat

  • Thermally Regulated Workwear for Field Operations
  • Alternative Cooling Solutions & Mobile Water Dispensing Solutions
  • Fire Hydrant Locking Devices & Adaptive Cooling Technologies

Field Operations

  • Field-Based Data Collection Tools
  • Ground-Level Street Imagery

Cybersecurity

  • Cybersecurity for Operational Technology (OT) & SCADA Networks
  • AI Usage Monitoring, Governance & Risk Mitigation

Modelling

  • Water Demand & Wastewater Flow Modeling
  • Advanced Weather Modeling for Stormwater Risk

Capital Planning and Data

  • Materials Cost Tracking & Estimation
  • Automated Triage for Chemical Safety Filings
  • Strategic Planning & Road Mapping Tool
  • Cross-Team KPI & Metrics Tracking
  • Inquiry & Complaint Tracking System
2

Asset Condition & Failure Prevention

The challengeHow can DEP detect problems in its pipes, equipment, and infrastructure earlier, so it can act before failures, breaks, or downtime?

DEP's pipes, pumps, floodgates, and aqueducts are aging and mostly hidden underground or underwater. This challenge is about spotting trouble early — catching a leak, crack, or failing pump before it becomes a water-main break, a flood, or a shutdown.

The four areas inside it

Pipe, Main, and Aqueduct Integrity

  • Deep Rock Aqueduct Leak Detection & Surface Tracking
  • Predictive Tool to Detect Water Main Breaks
  • Alternative Tools to Detect Leaks
  • Non-Destructive Force Main Inspection

Treatment Plant and Process Health

  • Equipment Monitoring Sensors
  • Continuous Monitoring of Wastewater/Sewer Parameters
  • Predictive Maintenance for Pumps & Blowers

Coastal Resilience

  • Contact Sensors for Floodgate Status Monitoring
  • Crack & Shift Detection for Flood Walls
  • Floodgate Opening/Closing Technology

Water Supply and Environmental Field Monitoring

  • Cloudburst/GI Monitoring
  • Sample Line Biofouling Mitigation
  • Dam Safety Data Acquisition & Trend Tracking
3

Resource & Energy Recovery

The challengeHow can DEP capture more value from its wastewater and sewer systems, recovering usable energy, heat, and materials?

This challenge treats wastewater as a resource, not just waste. As it flows through the sewers it carries heat, energy, and useful materials — this is about capturing that value instead of letting it go down the drain.

The two areas inside it

Measurement and Planning

  • Flow & Temperature Monitoring
  • Energy & Decarbonization Planning Tools

Recovery and Conversion

  • On-site Dewatering Technology
  • Nutrient Recovery
  • Non-Invasive Sewer Heat Recovery
  • Alternative Biogas Use
