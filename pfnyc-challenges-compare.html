DEP's three 2026 challenges

You'll choose one challenge and keep it for the whole program. Meet all three, then claim yours in the final step of Week 1. There is no best one — pick the problem you'd most want to live in.

1. Planning, Data & Field Operations

The planning & paperwork side of a giant utility. How can DEP make planning, cost estimation, field data collection, and administrative review faster and more data-driven? Five areas: Extreme Heat & Crew Safety, Field Ops & Imagery, Cybersecurity (OT/SCADA), Modelling, Capital Planning & Data. Good pick if you like data, software, planning, cost, security, or worker safety — the widest menu.

2. Asset Condition & Failure Prevention

Catching failure before it happens. How can DEP detect problems in its pipes, equipment, and infrastructure earlier — before failures, breaks, or downtime? Four areas: Pipe/Main/Aqueduct Integrity, Treatment-Plant & Process Health, Coastal Resilience, Water Supply & Environmental Monitoring. Good pick if you like sensors, inspection, prediction, or condition monitoring.

3. Resource & Energy Recovery

Getting value out of wastewater. How can DEP capture more value from its wastewater and sewers — recovering energy, heat, and materials? Two areas: Measurement & Planning, Recovery & Conversion. Good pick if you like clean energy, chemistry, sustainability, or novel hardware — the newest and least-crowded challenge.
