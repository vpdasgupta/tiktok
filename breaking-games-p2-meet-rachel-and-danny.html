Meet Rachel and Danny

R

Rachel, 38

The current buyer

Who
Two kids, 8 and 11. Hosts game night with the neighbors about once a month. Buys games as gifts.
Where they scroll
Instagram and Facebook, in the evening
Moment
A rainy Saturday or a family birthday. She wants one game the kids and grandparents can all play.
Pain point
No patience for a long rulebook. Has bought games that sat on the shelf. Knows a few games, but not the company.
Win
Saves the post, taps through to the new site, buys, and at the end of the night everyone says "good game."
Best-fit games
  • Caminito
  • The Game of 49
  • Play2Read
  • POOP
D

Danny, 21

New, younger players

Who
College junior and a true game lover. Hosts game night most Fridays: party games when new people come over, a big strategy game when it's just the regulars.
Where they scroll
TikTok, between classes
Moment
Planning a game night for 8 people, half of whom don't know each other. Or picking the next big strategy game for his regulars.
Pain point
Doesn't know which game works for a mixed group, or which big game is worth the money. Has never heard of Breaking Games. Skips anything that looks like an ad.
Win
Comments his answer, saves, taps the link, and remembers "Breaking Games."
