Extern house rules

Scroll or swipe across: Do, then Don’t.

Do

  • Follow the suggested pacing to stay on track and meet deadlines.
  • Join company meetings and program manager check-ins.
  • Use Slack or your program manager for all questions and updates.
  • Submit work that meets the quality standards in the training pages.
  • Follow the official guidance when posting about or showcasing your externship.

Don’t

Every Extern program

  • Contact company executives, managers or other employees directly unless invited through official channels.
  • Ask company representatives for jobs, internships or referrals.
  • Send unsolicited emails, messages or connection requests to anyone at the host company.
  • Misrepresent your role. You’re an extern hosted by Extern, Inc. in partnership with Breaking Games, not an intern or employee.
  • List Breaking Games as your employer on LinkedIn or your résumé.
  • Share confidential project information, data or deliverables outside approved channels.

Just for this program

  • Post program work anywhere: not Breaking Games’ accounts, and not your own. Upload it to the Video Board instead.
  • Contact creators, retailers or customers.
  • Use or tag @breakinggames on Instagram. It’s an unrelated person. The official accounts are TikTok @breakinggamesllc and Instagram @breaking_games.
  • Roast the person. Roast the post instead, and keep raw roast notes inside the program.
