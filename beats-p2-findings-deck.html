Step 1 of 6

Set up your Findings tab

  1. Click + at the bottom left of your Sheet. Name the tab Findings.
  2. In A1, write your Sample line: number of responses, your 4 ads and your dates.
  3. In row 3, type the 5 headers: Finding, Evidence, Quote, Backed up / Challenged / One source, Beats could…
Sample (made up)

Sample: 12 responses (14 in, 2 dropped) · Beats_YouTube_01, Beats_TikTok_01, Skullcandy_TikTok_01, Sony_YouTube_01 · Oct 6–9

You're on track if… Your Sample line has a number, 4 ad names and dates.

If you're stuck… Choose View > Freeze > 3 rows so the headers stay put.

Step 2 of 6

Pick your 3 findings

Reread your 3 research questions from Step 1. Then scan your Results tab for clear gaps (over 0.5), split votes and codes that show up again and again.

  • Answers a questionEach finding speaks to one of your research questions.
  • About BeatsAt least 2 of the 3 say something about a Beats ad.
  • A real gapSkip ties under 0.2. They aren't findings yet.

You're on track if… You have 3 short notes, one per finding, before you write anything polished.

If you're stuck… Start with Beats' strongest and weakest attribute from your Results tab.

Step 3 of 6

Write the Finding column

One or two sentences. Number + comparison + so what.

Sample (made up)

Stars get attention, not trust. Beats_YouTube_01 scored highest on attention (4.5) but lowest on believable (3.0). In my 12 responses, the star got people to look but not to believe.

You're on track if… A classmate could read it and know the ad, the number and why it matters.

If you're stuck… Start with a short bold line that says the idea in 5 words. Then add the numbers.

Step 4 of 6

Add Evidence and a Quote

Evidence is the numbers from your Results tab. Quote is one exact answer from your 6 quotes.

Sample (made up)
Evidence
Attention 4.5 vs. believable 3.0. 8 of 12 coded "Feels like an ad." 7 of 12 picked it as most memorable, 1 of 12 as most believable.
Quote
"Cool, but I'd never buy headphones because of that."

You're on track if… Every number in Evidence matches your Results tab.

If you're stuck… No quote fits? Leave the cell blank rather than force one.

Step 5 of 6

Label how sure you are

Did a second source agree? If you did the optional comment check, compare. If not, write "One source."

Sample (made up)
Finding 1
Backed up: 6 of 10 comments on Beats_YouTube_01 were about the athlete, not the headphones.
Finding 2
One source (survey).

You're on track if… Each row has one label and, if backed up or challenged, says by what.

If you're stuck… Comments disagree with your survey? Write "Challenged" and keep the finding. That's honest, not wrong.

Step 6 of 6

Write the "Beats could…" line

One idea a creative team could try. Name what changes in the ad.

Sample (made up), all 3 findings
1 · Stars get attention, not trust
Beats could pair the star with a real fan.
2 · Creators earn trust
Beats could test more creator-led TikToks.
3 · Tech facts are trusted but forgotten
Beats could put one product fact inside a story.

You're on track if… Each line is specific enough to sketch an ad from it.

If you're stuck… "Be more authentic" is too vague. Ask: what would we see on screen?
