Level 1 of 3: The briefing

Breaking Games in 3 cards

🎲

Who they are

An indie tabletop game publisher in Netcong, New Jersey. It makes party, family and strategy games, and helps independent designers get their games made and into players' hands.

It launched at Toy Fair in 2015, and its site calls it "an indie game publishing division of AdMagic."

🤝

"Good game, folks."

The new brand line. It's about connection, playing face to face, and making memories over a game. Fun and warm, not edgy.

Show people choosing the table; never mock phones or screens.

🧩

Caminito comes first

Caminito is the brand-new game, and it leads the relaunch. Its price, ages and player count are coming from Breaking Games. Your program manager will share them.

Until then, don't guess any Caminito facts.
