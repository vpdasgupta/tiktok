Level 1 of 3: The briefing

Breaking Games in 4 cards

🎲

Who they are

Game producers in Netcong, New Jersey. In their words: "We're game producers who work with designers, making and marketing original games that rise to any occasion and leave everyone feeling like a winner."

Designers bring the idea. Breaking Games handles the rest, from refining the gameplay to making the pieces to getting the game on shelves.

🤝

"Good game, folks."

The new tagline. "Good game" is what you say when everyone showed up, played their part and had fun together. "Folks" is warm, like a 90s game-show host talking to neighbors.

The mission: bring people together over great games, good company and old-fashioned fun. Show people choosing the table; never mock phones or screens.

🎤

How they sound

A charismatic emcee, not a scene-stealing performer. Witty and warm, but the game is always the star.

Casual, not sloppy. Friendly, not salesy. Occasionally ridiculous, never unhinged. Periods over exclamation points, and PG.

🧩

Caminito comes first

Caminito is the brand-new game, and it leads the relaunch. Its price, ages and player count are coming from Breaking Games. Your program manager will share them.

Until then, don't guess any Caminito facts.
