How to play

Five games to know before you post

Breaking Games is putting five games front and center: Dwellings of Eldervale, We're Doomed!, Mixtape, Hypothetically Fun and the brand-new Caminito. Move your token around the board to meet each one.

  • Start on House rules: the 3 checks every game post has to pass.
  • Land on a game: see who it's for, what to post and what to watch for.
  • Pitch it: pick the hook Breaking Games would post. Each wrong hook breaks one house rule.

Each right pitch earns 1 point, 5 in all. Your first pick counts, and every pick tells you why. It takes about 8 minutes.

    House rules

    Before any game goes in a post, it has to pass 3 checks. Every pitch on this board breaks one of them, or none.

    1. Every fact comes from the product page. Copy price, ages, players and play time. Never guess. If a fact isn't public yet, leave it out.
    2. It fits the table. Match the game to who's playing: their ages, the group size and the kind of night.
    3. It sounds like Breaking Games. A charismatic emcee: warm and witty, with periods over exclamation points. Say "game lovers," not "gamers," and never knock phones or screens.
