Project 2 — Concept Blueprint

Cart on the hdPhone

Prepared by: A. Sample · Lane: Customer Experience

1. Main Point

If an associate's hdPhone can pull up a customer's online cart, Pro customers like Marcus can finish the order they started at home without rebuilding it by hand in the aisle. The idea depends most on one hand-off: the hdPhone finding the online order. My prototype will show the simplest version: see the cart in the aisle, pay at the Pro desk.

2. The Concept

Idea: An associate scans a code on Marcus's phone, and his saved cart appears on the associate's hdPhone.

Why this shape: Mostly tech (the hdPhone shows the cart), plus a people change: associates learn to ask Pros for their order code.

Evidence: I built a cart in the Home Depot app myself, and associates already carry 125,000+ hdPhones for scanning and stock checks (Home Depot newsroom, 2022). Lowe's put an AI helper on associates' devices across 1,700+ stores, so associate devices work at home-improvement scale. That doesn't prove a device can show a customer's cart; that part is still a guess. So is how often items get missed.

Set aside: Scan at hello, because it needs a greeter at the door. Pay in the aisle, because payment questions can come later.

5. Risks & Versions

  • Will it work? Only if the hdPhone can reach online orders. That's the biggest guess.
  • Will people use it? Busy associates may skip the scan, and Pros may not know to offer their code.
  • What does it cost? Software work to connect the systems, plus a few minutes of associate training.

Simplest version: The associate sees Marcus's cart on the hdPhone in the aisle, and he pays at the Pro desk as he does today.

Ideal version: Marcus pays right there in the aisle on the associate's device. Guess: this needs payment on the hdPhone.

My prototype will show: the simplest version, because it fixes the missed items without the payment questions.

The tradeoff: My simplest version gives up paying in the aisle to get fewer missed items without new payment technology.

6. What Home Depot Would Need to Confirm

  1. Can the hdPhone show a customer's online cart today?
  2. Can OrderUp! at the Pro desk load an order started on an hdPhone?
