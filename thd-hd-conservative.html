Conservative

The Home Depot moves in careful, tested steps and tends to lean on what has worked before rather than betting big on the untried. A pitch that asks for a sweeping change reads as risky; one that starts small reads as smart.

  • Frame it as a pilot. Propose testing your idea in one store, one aisle, or one product category first, with a plan to expand only if it works.
  • Point to precedent. Note a time the idea (or a close cousin) already worked, so it feels proven rather than experimental.
