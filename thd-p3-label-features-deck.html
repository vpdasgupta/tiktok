Know Your Smart Label Features & Best Practices: label features

Label feature 1 of 6

Multi-price display

Two or more prices on one label

Lever 1

What it does
one screen shows more than one price, for example the hanging display fan and the boxed fan, or a pack price and a single-item price. MET asked for this for "mama-baby" products sold both ways.
In the Chroma range?
Yes. Displaydata says its screens can show up to 6 prices at once [vendor].
Price per item
the same as a standard label ($5–$13). One label now covers 2 tags, so the price per tag is cut in half.
Lever
1 (paper tags replaced per smart label).
Proof
vendor claim only; no named retailer result found.
Watch out
pricing rules. A 2026 draft model rule would set a minimum text height for unit prices on electronic labels [independent]. Ask MET whether one label can stand for two spots.

Label feature 2 of 6

Color display

Red, or red and yellow

Lever 1

What it does
shows the price and a color cue, like a yellow clearance tag. MET asked for color.
In the Chroma range?
Yes, a four-color Chroma exists [vendor].
Price per item
near the top of the standard range, about $13 each, and up to $15–$50+ in some guides [vendor].
Lever
1, if color replaces a separate clearance or promo tag. It also raises lever 2 (costs more).
Proof
Mitre 10 (New Zealand home improvement) runs a 45,000-item four-color install [vendor]. Tesco is rolling color labels out to about 3,000 UK stores from June 2026 [independent].
Watch out
Taylor said color models were "far less durable" when Home Depot tested them. Color screens also refresh slower (about 15 seconds vs. 2 for black and white) [vendor].

Label feature 3 of 6

Large-format / feature display

About 5 to 12 inches

Levers 1 + 3

What it does
shows price plus features, specs or a promo.
In the Chroma range?
Yes. Home Depot already uses large-format Chroma labels in appliances.
Price per item
about $16–$38 for 6–7.5 inches; $50+ for 10 inches or more [vendor].
Lever
1 (one screen can replace a price tag and a feature card, or two tags) and 3 (a big card takes longer to change by hand). It raises lever 2.
Proof
Lowe's put SoluM labels in all U.S. stores by December 2020, starting with appliances; no results published [independent].
Watch out
check the price against your ceiling first. A bigger target in bulky aisles.

Label feature 4 of 6

Thin / flush-mount profile

Sticks out less from the shelf

Lever 4

What it does
less to catch on carts and pallets. At the October 7 meeting, MET praised Jaquan's thin-label idea — in effect, "good that somebody thought of that, because we didn't."
In the Chroma range?
Not found. The thinnest models we found come from other makers, as thin as 4.5 mm (about 8–10 mm is more common) [vendor]. So this one may need a different model or vendor: count that as a risk.
Price per item
not published separately; assume the standard $7–$13 until quoted.
Lever
4 (fewer knocks, if true).
Proof
no named retailer result found.
Watch out
no public data proves thinner breaks less, and a thinner body may hold a smaller battery. Ask the vendor for replacement data by model.

Label feature 5 of 6

Rugged (sealed) build or a guard

Lever 4

What it does
sealed against water and dust, or protected by bumper rails and covers. Built for garden centers, freezers and bottom shelves.
In the Chroma range?
Partly. A Chroma freezer model exists (black and white only) [vendor]. Guards and bumper rails are add-ons that fit the shelf edge, whatever the label.
Ratings
IP67 and IP68 are water-and-dust ratings (IP68 survives longer underwater). Bumper rails are sold to protect shelves "against impact from trolleys, pallets" [vendor].
Price per item
not found. Test it as a labeled assumption (+$2? +$5?).
Lever
4. Slightly raises lever 2.
Proof
no measured before-and-after damage rates found anywhere.
Watch out
without a measured damage rate, show what happens if a guard halves your damage — and label it an assumption.

Label feature 6 of 6

LED light, NFC or buttons

Lever 7

What it does
a light flashes to point workers to a spot (restocking, picking online orders, putting returns back). NFC lets a phone tap the label.
In the Chroma range?
LED: yes, on sizes up to the Chroma 37 [vendor].
Price per item
not found separately; expect the top of the $5–$15 range.
Lever
7 (extra labor saved).
Proof
Leroy Merlin cut picking time 17% for 2-hour pickup [independent]. Walmart uses "stock to light" and "pick to light" [independent].
Watch out
your model counts only price-change labor. To use lever 7, estimate the hours saved, label it an estimate, and ask whether MET or another team does that work.
