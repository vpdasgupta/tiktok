Step 2 · Match threats to elements

The Four Smart Label Elements

Every smart label is built from the same four physical elements — and each one is what stands between a specific threat and a working label. Two labels can show the same price yet survive very differently, because their elements are built differently. Match the elements to the bay.

  • 1📐 How it mounts

    Flush against the beam and out of the strike path — or sticking out where a fork or cart hits it first.

  • 2🛡️ What protects it

    The casing: a rugged housing and bumpers that absorb hits — or bare plastic edges that crack.

  • 3🖥️ The screen

    The display: shatter-resistant and readable in bright light — or fragile glass that washes out.

  • 4🔌 The electronics

    Sealing, battery, and connection that keep it alive — or let dust, damp, and dead radios in.

Element 1 of 4

1

How it mounts

flush vs. sticking out

Holds up when…The label sits flush against the beam, out of the strike path.
Gives way when…The label sticks out — the first thing a fork, a ladder, or a cart hits.

Element 2 of 4

2

What protects it

the casing

Holds up when…A rugged housing or bumper absorbs and deflects strikes, drops, and bumps.
Gives way when…Bare plastic and exposed edges crack on first contact.

Element 3 of 4

3

The screen

the display

Holds up when…The screen resists shattering and reads clearly after knocks and in bright light.
Gives way when…A fragile glass screen chips or spiders, or washes out in sunlight.

Element 4 of 4

4

The electronics

sealing, battery, connection

Holds up when…The housing is sealed against dust and moisture (look for an IP rating), with a long-life battery and a robust connection.
Gives way when…Unsealed electronics let dust and damp in; a weak battery or flaky radio makes it freeze or show a stale price.
