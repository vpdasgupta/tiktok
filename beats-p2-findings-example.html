The 3 research questions these answer

  1. Do celebrity hooks make Beats ads more believable, or just more noticeable?
  2. Is a creator-led TikTok more believable than a celebrity ad?
  3. Are tech-focused ads as easy to remember as people-led ads?

Why this example works

  • The Sample line gives the number of responses, the 4 ads and the dates.
  • Each finding has a number, a comparison and a so what, and answers a different research question.
  • 2 of the 3 findings are about a Beats ad. Every gap is over 0.5, so none rests on a tie.
  • Every number matches the Results tab, and every quote is copied word for word from the Quotes list.
  • The labels are honest: one finding is backed up, one is challenged and kept, and one has only the survey behind it.
  • With 12 responses, the findings say “in my 12 responses” and “suggests” instead of “proves.”
  • Each “Beats could…” line names something you'd see in the ad.
