Your Ad Repository

One row per ad, in three quick passes

Each ad should take a few minutes, not twenty.

  • 1 · The basics (A–G)What it is and where you found it
  • 2 · What made it work (H–M)Hook, talent, CTA, sound and production, all picked from dropdowns
  • 3 · Your take (N–O)A score and a sentence or two

Tip: The Taxonomy Reference tab in your sheet defines every option.

Pass 1 · Columns A–G

The basics

A: Ad ID
Brand_Platform_##, e.g. Beats_TikTok_01
B: Brand
Beats, Bose, and so on
C: Platform
TikTok, Instagram or YouTube
D: Ad Format
e.g. In-Feed, Reels. Unsure? Use where you found it
E: Duration (sec)
Length in whole seconds
F: Capture Date
The day you found the ad
G: Video File Link
Paste the ad's own link. No recording or upload

Pass 2 · Column H

Hook Type: what made you keep watching?

The hook is whatever grabs you in the first 1–3 seconds.

  • QuestionOpens with a question, on screen or spoken
  • Problem/Pain PointOpens on a frustration, like a dead battery
  • TransformationA before and after, or a sudden change
  • Movement/ActionFast motion or quick cuts
  • Celebrity/InfluencerA face you recognize shows up first
  • Sound/MusicThe audio pulls you in before the visuals
  • Other/CompoundTwo hooks hit equally hard, or none fits

Pass 2 · Column I

Talent Strategy: who's in the ad?

  • CelebrityA famous athlete, musician or actor
  • Creator/InfluencerA social creator, often talking to camera
  • Real People (UGC-Style)Everyday-looking people, often phone-shot
  • Brand CharacterA mascot or recurring character
  • No Talent (Product Only)Just the product, no people
  • MixedTwo or more, with equal screen time

Pass 2 · Column J

CTA Type: what does the ad ask you to do?

  • Direct Purchase"Shop Now," "Buy Now"
  • Learn More"Learn More," "Explore"
  • Get OfferA discount or deal
  • Download/InstallAsks you to get an app
  • No Explicit CTAEnds on the logo, no ask
  • Implicit CTAA soft nudge, like a creator saying where they bought it

Pass 2 · Column K

CTA Placement: where does the ask show up?

  • End CardOn the final frame
  • ThroughoutVisible for most of the ad
  • Voice OnlySpoken but never shown
  • Text OverlayPops up as text partway through
  • Platform NativeThe platform's own button under the video
  • NoneNo CTA at all (pairs with No Explicit CTA)

Pass 2 · Column L

Sound Design: what carries the ad?

  • Licensed MusicA recognizable song
  • Original MusicA track made for the ad
  • Trending SoundOne you've heard on other TikToks or Reels
  • Voice OverA narrator you don't see
  • DialoguePeople talking on screen
  • ASMR/ProductProduct sounds up front, like case snaps
  • MixedSeveral sounds share the spotlight
  • Silent/MinimalLittle or no sound; works on mute

Pass 2 · Column M

Production Level: how polished does it look?

  • High (Studio)Cinematic, big-budget look
  • Medium (Professional)Polished but simpler
  • Low (UGC-style)Deliberately phone-shot
  • Native/OrganicLooks like a regular post on the platform

Two options fit? For any of H–M, pick the one that stands out most and mention the other in Key Observations.

Pass 3 · Column N

Effectiveness Score: 1 to 5

1 is weak, 5 is excellent. Use these questions as a gut check, then give one overall score. Decimals are fine.

  • Would I stop scrolling?
  • After 3 seconds, do I know what this is about?
  • Does it feel like the brand?
  • How did it make me feel?
  • Is it well made?

The hook matters most: if nobody stops scrolling, nothing else gets seen. Score lo-fi ads on whether they work, not on polish.

Pass 3 · Column O

Key Observations: 1–2 sentences

Note what was interesting, especially anything the dropdowns couldn't capture.

  • "Second hook at 0:02: bass drops right as the product appears."
  • "Feels like a regular creator post until the Shop Now button shows up."
  • "No talking at all, but the case-click sound does the selling."

Recap

Your routine for every ad

Log the hook (H)

Fill in talent, CTA, sound and production (I–M), plus the basics (B–F)
3 · Score it
Give it a 1–5 (N)
4 · Wrap up
Jot down what was interesting (O), add the Ad ID (A) and paste the link (G)
