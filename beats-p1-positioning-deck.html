3.2 Compare Competitive Positioning

Where does Beats stand in the market?

Step 1 · Profile

Write the last column in plain words

"Their ads say…" is one short line on what the brand wants you to believe.

  • Sony"Nothing sounds better or blocks more noise."
  • Apple AirPods"It just works with your iPhone."
  • Beats"This is what the people you admire wear."

Go by your ads, not what you already knew. If your data disagrees with 1.4 Meet the Competition, trust your data.

Step 2 · Map

Two questions place every brand

These match the questions you answered in 1.4 Meet the Competition.

Left to right
Does it sell on sound and tech, or on style and culture?
Top to bottom
Does it feel premium, or everyday?

Clues in your data: Problem/Pain Point hooks and product-only ads point to sound and tech. Celebrity, creator and music-led hooks point to style and culture. Studio production points to premium. UGC style and Get Offer CTAs point to everyday.

Keep it simple: A 2×2 table in your Google Doc is enough. No design tools needed.

Step 3 · Threat and opening

Name one risk and one chance for Beats

Each one needs a number from your sheet behind it.

Threat (made up)
Skullcandy's creator ads averaged 4.1, close to Beats' 4.2, at a lower price. Beats' style edge may be easier to copy than it looks.
Opening (made up)
No brand in my sample used a Question hook. Beats could test one to stand out in the feed.

A direction, not a campaign. You'll build campaign ideas in Project 2.
