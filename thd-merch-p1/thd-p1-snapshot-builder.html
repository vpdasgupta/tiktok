Anatomy of a First-Look Snapshot

A low-stakes warm-up. Write this snapshot from instinct before you learn the research method — a rough, honest first read is the whole point. Expand each part to see the prompt, guidance, and a short example.

The three parts of a snapshot

Guidance

Describe the company and its ESL product in plain English.

Example fragment

Chroma makes e-paper digital shelf labels — the ones Home Depot already runs on its shelves.

Guidance

Name the one thing that seems most impressive or useful for a retailer.

Example fragment

Its biggest strength looks like a crisp, low-power display that's easy to update remotely.

Guidance

Name the one thing that makes you nervous about this label surviving a Home Depot aisle full of forklifts and ladders.

Example fragment

My worry: the label seems to stick out from the beam — in a forklift aisle that feels like a durability problem I'd want to verify.

That "I'd want to verify…" instinct is the mindset the whole externship rewards.
