Project 1 Rubric + Glossary

How your work is scored

Three things carry your grade. Open each bar to see what Insufficient, Solid, and Excellent look like.

✕ Insufficient

Assumption-based; vendor marketing taken as fact; no sources; gaps not acknowledged.

~ Solid

Key claims sourced; some verification against primary sources; a few gaps named.

★ Excellent

Strong triangulation across primary sources; every damage and spec claim verified or explicitly flagged; gaps named precisely as next questions.

✕ Insufficient

Generic; reads like a traditional-retail ESL case; ignores warehouse constraints.

~ Solid

References warehouse constraints; recommendation is selective but light on feasibility.

★ Excellent

References MET's specific context (damage, forklift zones, the failed test) throughout AND addresses implementation feasibility.

✕ Insufficient

AI outputs used as-is; invented specs/rates/costs; no verification or judgment shown.

~ Solid

AI used to accelerate; most AI-surfaced claims checked against a source.

★ Excellent

AI used to accelerate, with visible documentation of where you verified, corrected, or overrode it — judgment is clearly yours.

Graded on your thinking, not your polish.
