YouTube

Google's ABCDs, in plain words

Attention
"Jump in." Get to the heart of the story faster, with tight framing. Back it up with audio and on-screen text.
Branding
"Brand early, often, and richly." Show the brand or product from the start and keep it there.
Connection
Feature people and keep the message simple.
Direction
End with a clear call to action, and say it out loud as well as on screen.

TikTok

Make TikToks, not TV ads

Made for TikTok
Use sound or music, shoot vertical 9:16 and keep key content inside the safe zone.
The look
Go for a DIY style that isn't overly polished. Feature people such as creators or customers.
The hook
Introduce what you're selling in the first 3 seconds, for better recall. Hook people within the first 6.
Captions
Use captions or text overlays to give context.

Meta (Instagram and Facebook)

Reels ads and mobile video

Reels basics
Vertical 9:16 video with quality audio. Reels play with sound on by default.
Safe zone
Keep key messages where buttons and captions won't cover them.
Hook and brand early
"Hook people right away." Bring in your brand early.
Sound off
"Build for sound off. Delight with sound on." Add captions, text and graphics.

Is this a strong source?

Check it fast with CRAAP

  • CurrencyWhen was it written? Meta's mobile video tips are older, so check them against the current Reels page.
  • RelevanceIs it about video ads on this platform?
  • AuthorityDid the platform write it, or someone quoting the platform?
  • Accuracy and PurposeCan you trace the claim? Is it trying to sell you something?

Rule of thumb: if a blog quotes "a study" with no link, find the platform page it came from or drop the claim.
