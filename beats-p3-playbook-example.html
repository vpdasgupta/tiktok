Beats Creative Strategy Framework

Alex Rivera · Project 3 · Oct 20

1. Platform Playbook

Sources read Oct 20: Google Ads Help (ABCDs), TikTok Ads Help (creative best practices), Meta for Business (Reels ads; mobile video tips).

For every platform

  1. Hook in the first 3 seconds. Google, TikTok, Meta.
    For Beats: 6 of 10 Beats ads in my repository open with a celebrity or influencer hook, and Beats_YouTube_01 scored 4.5 on attention in my survey. Beats hooks well. Keep it.
  2. Show or say the brand early. Google, TikTok, Meta. To fill in: the For Beats line, tied to an ad or number from your research.
  3. To fill in: rule 3 (it should be in all 3 sources), its sources and a For Beats line.

YouTube

  1. Jump in: get to the heart of the story fast, with tight framing. (Google ABCDs)
  2. End with a clear call to action, said out loud and shown on screen. (Google ABCDs)

TikTok

  1. Make it look made for TikTok: sound on, vertical 9:16, not overly polished. (TikTok creative best practices)
  2. To fill in: rule 2 and its source link.

Meta

To fill in: 2 rules for Reels or mobile video, each with a source link.
